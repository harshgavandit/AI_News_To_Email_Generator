## Observed root causes

The production snapshot was taken at 07:39 UTC. The last observed feed check was 03:47 UTC. Of 478 enabled sources, only 12 were fresh, 460 stale and six failed. A five-minute GitHub cron declaration did not imply five-minute execution: inspected workflow starts were hours apart. The most recent red workflow also contained a mail-job failure; that alone is not evidence that its feed-refresh job failed.

1. **Selection was editorially hard-coded.** A fixed Nvidia CEO feature excluded every other Nvidia headline while active. Earnings also received a negative score. Both mechanisms have been removed.
2. **Archive presence was confused with discoverability.** Official announcements and videos were excluded from headlines even when useful primary evidence. The default Reading Room window was 12 hours. High-volume publishers contributed only a small recent slice to selection.
3. **The source mix underrepresented financial and regional reporting.** Existing breadth across developer feeds did not cover specific financial desks, official news video channels, product feeds or dated developer changelogs.
4. **Classification missed entity-only headlines.** Standalone Gemini/Qwen titles and database companies were weak signals. All videos were treated as the video topic irrespective of a reviewed news channel's actual focus.
5. **Metadata discovery stopped too early.** The listing adapter required `.html` article paths and only considered the first eight links. Previously archived links at the top could block discovery farther down a publisher's section.
6. **Operational visibility did not show scheduler lag or event-type gaps.** Existing individual source health did not explain how much of the fast-news queue was overdue, or whether acquisitions/earnings/IPO coverage was represented in a recent sample.

## Changes implemented

- Added 23 reviewed integrations: CTech, Korea JoongAng Daily news sitemap, Claude's dated platform notes, 9to5Google, 9to5Mac, ThePrint technology, FT technology, WSJ technology/business, CNBC investing/markets, SCMP business, Globes, MongoDB, Microsoft Official Blog, Google Gemini, Qwen, PR Newswire technology, and Microsoft/Bloomberg/Reuters/CNBC/FT YouTube channels.
- Applied the real ingestion adapters to every candidate. All 23 accepted endpoints parsed successfully; two currently had no in-scope technology entries. Nikkei's candidate feed lacked usable publication dates and MongoDB's investor feed rejected the identified application client. Those two were **not** integrated. See [timestamped source validation](source-validation-2026-11-18.json).
- Introduced title-derived acquisition, IPO, earnings, outage, security, policy, release, funding and leadership signals. Importance decays with age. Shopping headlines remain excluded from headline/priority selection. Signals are explainable cues, not independent fact verification.
- Browser checks found ambiguous words in actual results. An AI/security "takeover" is not treated as an acquisition, and "raises concerns" is not a funding round. The multilingual PR Newswire stream is labeled as unspecified/mixed language instead of incorrectly claiming every entry is English.
- Replaced the fixed developing-story panel with recent matching publisher headlines. Two feeds belonging to the same company cannot create a false independent-source count. Shared company names alone do not merge different events.
- Retained significant recent stories in bounded per-publisher candidate sampling, expanded headline candidates, admitted relevant primary announcements and news videos, and used material-news ranking in the world edition and priority queue. Existing exact-link deduplication, strict cross-source matching, source attribution and personal filters remain in place.
- Bumped archive classification to version 4 so existing records receive corrected topic and source-eligibility fields. Existing article IDs and user records are preserved.
- Changed the default Reading Room window to 24 hours; explicit saved 12-hour links still work. Story cards show factual event-type badges. Older stories remain available through larger windows and archive search.
- Bounded metadata discovery now scans up to 48 approved links, fetches at most eight unknown articles per check, respects robots rules and keeps only public metadata. Official Markdown notes keep actual day-level publication precision rather than inventing an announcement time.
- Prioritized refresh work by cadence-relative delay while reserving a quarter of each bounded batch for oldest checks. Existing host concurrency, conditional RSS requests, retry/backoff, disabled-source state and database job leases remain enforced.
- Moved the five-minute GitHub schedule off the top of the hour, expanded bounded backlog draining, and added independently scheduled hourly Vercel fallback calls. Overlapping refreshes use the same database lease.
- Added `/api/discovery/ingestion` with observed scheduler status, due/deferred sources, overdue priority sources and p95 delay. Expanded the existing coverage endpoint and Sources page with sampled event-type gaps and actual sync backlog.

## Evidence and limits

The normal production [feed-only synchronization run](https://github.com/harshgavandit/AI_News_To_Email_Generator/actions/runs/44267396336) completed successfully at 08:33 UTC. Its final measurement was **495 fresh sources, zero stale, six failed and zero pending** out of 501 enabled sources; all 165 priority feeds met their target deadline, and measured p95 overdue time was zero. This is an observed recovery snapshot, not a promise that future scheduler starts will meet every target.

All 23 newly integrated sources completed a healthy production check. Archive queries during the rollout returned Firebolt (1), Anthropic IPO (64), Gemini tasks (10), linked billing (4), Microsoft Ignite (20), transparency (19), Nvidia earnings (51), Alibaba (32), and Microsoft outage (95). These are broad retained-query totals and include overlapping/contextual results; they are not nine exact-article or independent-verification guarantees. In particular, the free-tasks story and the official linked-billing notes were ingested through normal source synchronization rather than manual article inserts.

Exact-URL checks at that point found the supplied 9to5Google article, Microsoft keynote clip and ThePrint article. Other supplied destinations were not all retained: short feeds/listings had already rotated some originals out; X was deliberately replaced with the official dated docs, and the Telegraph publisher remained unsupported. Story-level coverage exists through the company announcement or other reporting, but the system must not claim those original URLs were indexed.

The remaining failed checks at 08:35 UTC were Tech in Asia (access denied), Hashnode discovery (API unavailable), VentureBeat RSS and sitemap (rate limited), one Microsoft GitHub release feed (fetch failure), and Project Zero (fetch failure). Retained articles remain usable; retries/backoff and alternate publishers preserve browsing. Denied access is not bypassed. The platform correctly continues reporting degraded aggregate readiness while such failures remain.

Offline regressions cover the nine event types, Nvidia earnings visibility, matching-event/publisher-family deduplication, financial-feed scope, official video classification, candidate starvation, database search, stable dated changelog IDs, extensionless metadata discovery after known links, refresh fairness/backoff and public backlog monitoring. The existing reader, AI, personalized brief and email suites are also run before release.

Source validation uses public RSS/Atom, publisher-declared news sitemaps, allowed metadata and official Markdown. It does not copy paywalled bodies, bypass denied access, or fabricate content. A successful feed can still omit a particular article after that article rotates out of the publisher's feed. News-sitemap retention is often shorter than the platform's archive retention. New integrations cannot retroactively guarantee all older exact URLs.

The scheduler's target cadence is not a latency SLA. [GitHub documents delayed or dropped scheduled runs](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/troubleshoot-workflows). Vercel Hobby daily cron entries have a scheduling window, so the hourly fallback reduces dependence on GitHub without guaranteeing minute-level starts. No paid infrastructure upgrade or API subscription was introduced. Actual post-release source health and supplied-story query results must be checked separately from a successful build.
