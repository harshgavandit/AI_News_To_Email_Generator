# Informa AI product review

The review covered the public desktop experience for Particle, Trace, and Feedly, plus first-party documentation for features behind sign-in or paid plans. Authenticated workspaces, subscriptions, and deliveries were outside the direct comparison.

| Platform | What works well | What Informa AI applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Particle | A persistent topic list and image-led cards make the feed easy to skim. Cards show how many articles cover each story. Opening a story reveals a concise overview, linked entities, and original publisher links. Particle also documents alternate summary formats, questions, and listening in its mobile product. [Public feed](https://particle.news/) · [Product walkthrough](https://particle.news/blog/introducing-particle-the-news-organized) | Open an article beside the feed with clear publisher provenance and the source excerpt. Make it easy to continue to the original source or browse the same topic. Show context when the brief actually supplies it. |
| Trace | The landing page leads with a time-bounded daily reading promise. Inside the brief, an editorial lead and table of contents support a quick read. Stories group related reporting and link the contributing publishers. The public site also describes topic preferences, email, and agent integrations. [Trace](https://www.yourtrace.online/) · [Public daily brief](https://www.yourtrace.online/brief/2026-11-16) | Give the Global Top 5 a clear path from preview to optional scheduled delivery. Read the same five stories aloud where supported. Keep links available in each edition and its exports. |
| Feedly | Source discovery uses a focused search field, source type choices, language filtering, and story previews. Feedly documents persistent feeds and boards, scoped search, AI Feeds for monitoring, and the Go To keyboard shortcut. [Public source discovery](https://feedly.com/i/discover) · [Search in Feedly](https://docs.feedly.com/article/78-how-can-i-search-within-my-feedly) · [Feeds and Boards](https://docs.feedly.com/article/805-feeds-and-boards) · [Go To](https://docs.feedly.com/article/853-navigate-feedly-faster-with-go-to) | Keep search, a compact list, and publisher filters close to the results. Save a named set of filters so readers can reopen a monitoring view with fresh stories. Keep article bookmarks and brief pins separate so each action has a clear effect. |

Informa AI pairs editorial typography and a forest green palette with information about publishers, languages, and feed health. Quick search makes frequent actions available by keyboard. The brief editor keeps configuration, preview, export, and delivery in one workflow. The public app works with existing linkable filters and requires inbox verification for delivery.

Saved views and story state are stored in the browser. Story details are grounded in an individual publisher record. Related topic links open the topic feed. Cross-publisher story clustering and shared monitoring workspaces remain gaps relative to the products reviewed.
