The Particle observations come from the public feed and its product introduction. App-only features are documented capabilities. The central lesson for Informa AI is to make supporting coverage easy to reach from a readable publisher record. [Public feed](https://particle.news/) · [Product introduction](https://particle.news/blog/introducing-particle-the-news-organized)

Trace's dated public edition makes its finite reading structure observable. Its homepage explains topic preferences, email customization, and agent access. Informa AI applies the daily catch-up pattern through a five-story selection drawn from current publisher records. [Homepage](https://www.yourtrace.online/) · [November 16 edition](https://www.yourtrace.online/brief/2026-11-16)

Feedly's current homepage and reader documentation target different levels of work. Its documented strength is the ability to return to a scope, select evidence, and use it in a deliverable. Informa AI applies persistent monitoring navigation and an explicit handoff from selected stories into a brief. [Current homepage](https://feedly.com/) · [Feeds and Boards](https://docs.feedly.com/article/805-feeds-and-boards) · [Search filters](https://docs.feedly.com/article/79-how-can-i-filter-my-search-results) · [Ask AI source selection](https://docs.feedly.com/article/854-how-ask-ai-and-research-select-articles)

The existing Informa AI interface repeated topic controls, put a large subscription area before discovery, and left a full brief configuration form in the reading flow. Saved views required reopening a small disclosure. Read state was visible but could not remove completed stories from the current selection. The live source review also found general world reporting under SCMP Technology.

The implementation now gives the workspace a persistent topic and monitoring navigation, a central reading area, and a contextual rail for trending subjects, developing coverage, and optional daily delivery. A compact masthead replaces the previous promotional hero. Instrument Serif headlines, Instrument Sans controls, forest green actions, and shared light/dark tokens establish Informa AI's own editorial treatment. Fonts are served locally with their OFL licenses.

Daily briefing is a complete reading view backed by the briefing API. It selects up to five current stories from reporting, official updates, advisories, journals, and preprints, diversifies publishers and subjects, and reduces repeated headline coverage. Each item keeps its original title, link, and excerpt. Publisher counts describe matches in the sampled archive. Readers can open a story, compare coverage, or pin the selection into the existing brief pipeline.

The brief builder opens as an accessible modal workspace on desktop and a sheet on smaller screens. It maintains keyboard dismissal, focus isolation, preview, exports, verification, and delivery controls. Hide read persists in browser storage and applies to the loaded selection. Saved monitoring views are accessible from the navigation and retain the existing filter representation. Bookmarks and interests remain browser-local.

The archive adds a source eligibility field and backfills existing rows. Reviewed path and title constraints therefore also govern retained stories. SCMP now uses its technology RSS endpoint. Ineligible legacy records stay retained while discovery excludes them. This change addresses search and recommendation quality alongside the interface.

Comparative visual validation remains limited by browser access. Shared team accounts, cross-device libraries, notifications for followed events, semantic retrieval, and question answering across a growing evidence set remain future product and operational work.
