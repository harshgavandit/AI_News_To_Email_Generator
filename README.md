# World Tech Brief

Browse recent technology news, research, service outages, and videos from publishers around the world. Filter by topic, region, source, or time. Share a filtered Reading Room link or pin specific stories to your brief.

Get the global top five or build a brief with your own interests, reader persona, and pinned stories. Preview it at full width or at 375 pixels, switch the email preview to dark mode, and copy Markdown or download HTML or an email file.

Verify an inbox to send up to three briefs per UTC day. Readers can opt in to a daily global brief around 8 AM in their local timezone. Verified readers can also send a brief to a Slack or Microsoft Teams webhook.
