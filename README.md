# World Tech Brief

World Tech Brief collects recent technology news from around the world. Browse company news, chip and device launches, policy, security, AI, and outages. Stories link back to their publishers, and official status updates are labeled separately from reporting.

Open the 24-hour brief for five stories across tech, or enter a subject for a personalized brief with AI summaries. Read it on the site, copy it, download it, or send it to your verified inbox. Email is free and on demand, with up to three briefs per inbox per UTC day and no subscription.
