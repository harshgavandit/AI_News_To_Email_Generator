Your reading lists stay in this browser.

Saved stories, read markers, interests, followed lists, named monitoring views, theme, and reading layout are stored in your browser. They are not synced to a reader account. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to see them.

Selected preferences are sent to the service when needed to retrieve recommendations. Browser storage is different from essential email-session cookies. Clearing reading lists here does not sign you out of email or delete server-side records.

Search and AI use the information needed for the request.

Search terms and filters are sent to Informa AI to find matching articles. Shared story and monitoring URLs can contain those filters, so review a link before sharing it.

When you request AI analysis or a brief, selected article titles and excerpts and the instructions you submit may be sent to OpenAI. Semantic search may also send your query to OpenAI. Avoid including sensitive personal information. Informa AI does not save your research questions or answers in its archive.

Browser-only topic watches send their keywords to Informa AI while the Research Desk is open. Optional background monitoring stores keywords on the server so it can check the archive between visits. The monitor uses publisher records directly.

The package checker sends the ecosystem, package name, and version you enter to OSV. Only enter public package names.

Technology watch drafts may send the description you enter to OpenAI. The app saves a rule only after you review and save its filters.

Research projects use your background-monitoring session. Collaborators can see the question, selected sources, and project notes. Publishing a brief creates a link that includes the question and public source evidence. Disable background monitoring to delete projects you own and withdraw their links.

Email and author features are optional.

Inbox verification uses your email address and a short-lived code. Verification alone does not subscribe you. Sending a brief or opting into scheduled delivery shares your address and message with the configured email provider. Scheduled delivery also stores your chosen timezone.

Story follows, viewed revisions, and recommendation feedback stay in this browser unless you choose to save your Tech Radar to a verified inbox. Saved Radar preferences and previously delivered story versions help personalize morning briefs you have opted into.

Email verification cookies expire after ten minutes for a pending challenge and seven days for a verified session. Verification cookies are restricted to the email feature. Unsubscribe using the link in a scheduled brief; signing out alone does not unsubscribe you.

If you submit an article, your name, verified email, submitted text, and permission evidence are retained for owner review. Approved article text and author attribution become public. Email and permission evidence remain private to the review process. You can withdraw reading permission in the author workspace; review records remain in the private archive.

The services involved.

  • Hosting: Vercel serves the site and processes requests. Hosting and security operations may retain request information, including network addresses and URLs, independently of optional analytics.
  • AI: OpenAI processes material used for AI briefs, research answers, and semantic search.
  • Email and bot protection: Configured delivery providers handle verification and requested messages. Cloudflare Turnstile may process browser signals when the email feature asks for a bot check.
  • Optional analytics: Vercel Web Analytics loads only with your choice enabled and no browser privacy signal requesting it be disabled. Its provider describes it as cookie-free. See Vercel’s analytics privacy information ↗.
  • Publishers: Opening original links takes you to another service. Some article thumbnails are loaded from publisher or image hosts, which receive the image request. Their own privacy practices apply.

Practical protection, with honest limits.

Production traffic uses HTTPS. The application restricts owner actions, checks incoming requests, and sanitizes displayed content. Email verification uses restricted session cookies. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Browser reading data stays until you remove it or your browser clears it. Routine cleanup removes news and cached summaries older than 90 days, delivery records older than 30 days, and source-check history older than seven days. Short-lived verification and preview records are cleaned after expiry. Cleanup runs periodically rather than at an exact deletion instant.

Subscriptions, delivery-suppression records, and permission-review records have separate operational purposes and are not removed by the browser reset. For access or removal requests involving server-side records, contact the creator. Hosting and service-provider retention may differ.
