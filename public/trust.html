Reviewed sources, visible attribution.

Sources are integrated through reviewed feeds, public APIs, and permitted connections. The directory includes established newsrooms, official company and engineering blogs, security advisories, research publications, and developer communities.

A reviewed source is a starting point for trust, not a guarantee about every claim it publishes. We do not independently fact-check every story or promise complete coverage. Reporting can change, and even reputable publishers can make mistakes.

Different sources serve different purposes.

Independent reporting

Journalism and analysis from publications. Open the original to check its evidence, qualifications, and later corrections.

Official announcements & advisories

First-hand statements from companies, project maintainers, and security organizations. An organization’s own announcement reflects its perspective.

Research & community writing

Papers, preprints, tutorials, and public posts. Research labels help distinguish preprints from journals; community inclusion does not imply peer review or editorial endorsement.

Freshness is visible. Gaps are acknowledged.

Feeds are checked on a schedule. The source directory shows synchronization status, check history, and the latest retained story. Failed or rate-limited connections keep their available archive while later checks retry.

A successful check means the connection worked. It does not mean every article from that publisher was included. Feeds rotate, sources restrict access, and some updates arrive late. Search supports up to 90 days of retained stories. Use “Can’t find a story?” in the directory to check a specific original link.

AI explanations stay distinguishable from reporting.

Publisher excerpts, headline-only records, extractive highlights, and AI-written briefs are labeled. AI briefs use the supplied article material, which may be limited to an excerpt. They can miss context or make mistakes; the original publisher remains the reference for its complete reporting.

Priority signals and trending topics help discovery. They are not guarantees that a development has been independently verified.

Respect for publishers and creators.

Informa AI links to original reporting and displays available excerpts. Full-text reading is offered only when a rights holder’s permission has been reviewed and approved. Paywalls and subscription requirements remain with the original publisher.

Authors can submit reading permission, track review, and withdraw that permission from the author workspace.
