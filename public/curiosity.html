How cards are chosen

  • Classics are reviewed by hand: each link is checked and the summary describes only what the source says.
  • Fresh cards come from engineering blogs already collected on our regular schedule. They are ranked for technical depth, announcements and duplicates are removed, and no company fills the shelf.
  • Summaries are labelled as curated, publisher excerpt or AI summary of the excerpt. Technologies are listed only when the title, excerpt or reviewed tags name them.
  • We never reproduce articles. Every card links to the original publisher, where the full story lives.
